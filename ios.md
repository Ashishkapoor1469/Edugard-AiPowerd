# Install EduGuard on iPhone or iPad

EduGuard can be added to the Home Screen from Safari. No App Store download is required.

1. Open Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
2. Visit [EduGuard](https://edugard-ai-powerd.vercel.app/).
3. Tap the **Share** button.
4. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
5. Confirm the name **EduGuard** and tap **Add**.
6. Open EduGuard from its icon on the Home Screen.

## If Add to Home Screen is missing

- Open the link directly in Safari, not Chrome or an in-app browser.
- Scroll down in the Share sheet and tap **Edit Actions** if necessary.
- Reload the page and try again.

## Remove the app

Long-press the EduGuard icon, tap **Remove App**, then **Delete Bookmark**. Your EduGuard account and college records are not deleted.

> This is the installable web version of EduGuard. An App Store release requires a separate Capacitor iOS build, Apple signing, and App Store review.
